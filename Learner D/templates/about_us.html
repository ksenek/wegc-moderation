About this guide

What this website provides

This guide offers organised, easy‑to‑read details about various bird species. Each profile I write focuses on key aspects such as body size, natural habitat and feeding behavior to help you identify and learn about them quickly.

The website itself is designed to be simple, clear and accessible for anyone wanting to explore the world of wild birds. My goal is to remove the confusion of dense scientific text, creating a welcoming space where you can easily find the information you need to appreciate the different variety of birds outside.
